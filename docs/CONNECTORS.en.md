# Connect Google Drive, mail and calendar

[Русская версия](CONNECTORS.md) · [Back to README](../README.en.md)

Teo works with Google Drive, Google Calendar and other services through Claude connectors. A connector is a service you connect inside your Claude account. Mail is connected in Teo separately.

The Teo interface is in Russian for now, so the Russian names of buttons and blocks are given in brackets.

## Google Drive, Google Calendar and other services: four steps

1. Open https://claude.ai/code. You can do it with the "Open claude.ai/code" button («Открыть claude.ai/code») in Teo: Dashboard, Connections («Подключения»).
2. On the left, choose "Customize".
3. Open "Connectors". Next to Google Drive press "+", then "Connect to Claude". Google Calendar connects the same way: press "+" next to Google Calendar.
4. Go back to Teo. It notices the connected services by itself. The "Check the list" button («Проверить список») does it faster.

Teo reads your connectors again and shows which ones are connected. Each service has a mark: "Teo uses it" («Тео пользуется») or "Teo does not use it" («Тео не пользуется»). You can change it.

## Mail

Gmail, Yandex Mail, Mail.ru and other mail: Teo searches and reads your emails. It needs an app password, not the main password of your mailbox.

1. Open your mail settings and create a password for apps. Gmail and Yandex call it an "app password".
2. In Teo: Connections, the "Other mail" block («Другая почта»). Enter your email address.
3. Paste the app password and press "Connect" («Подключить»).

## If you do not have Claude yet

Teo setup (the first-run wizard) takes you to the Claude sign-in. After that, the same four steps work.

## Actions inside services

By default Teo only reads. The "Allow actions in connections" switch («Разрешить действия в подключениях») turns actions on. Every action goes through a confirmation card with "Yes" and "No". Teo does not make payments or money transfers, even after a "yes".

## Telegram and WhatsApp

Sign in with a QR code or a phone number. Connections, the Telegram and WhatsApp blocks.

## The Teo browser

A separate browser with its own profile. Your sign-ins in it are stored on this computer only. Teo can do anything you ask in it, on your behalf. Teo warns you about this before it opens the browser for the first time.

You choose which browser Teo works in under Connections, on the "Teo browser" card («Браузер Тео»). By default it is the main browser of your system. Chrome, Edge and Yandex Browser work.
