# Roadmap

[Русская версия](ROADMAP.md) · [Back to README](../README.en.md)

Directions, not promised dates. The order changes based on what beta testers tell us.

## Now: beta 4

- Windows 10 and 11, macOS 13 or newer on Apple Silicon.
- A Dashboard with five buttons: Home, Work, Skills, Connections, Settings.
- The "Male 5" on-device voice («Мужской 5»), works offline. ElevenLabs with your own key.
- Sign in with email and password, or with Telegram.
- Claude connectors (Google Drive, Google Calendar and others), mail with an app password, WhatsApp and Telegram by QR code or phone number, the Teo browser with a choice of browser.
- Projects: Teo builds a page from your brief.
- A new version shows up as a card on Home.
- Teo saves your Claude usage limits.
- Errors and feedback go to the developers. This is how we fix what gets in your way.

## Next

- **Signed installers.** Today Windows and macOS warn about an unknown publisher. If many testers get stuck on this warning, we will add signing.
- **Faster answers.** We will shorten the pause before Teo answers.
- **Weak computers.** The light mode already exists. We will keep making it lighter.
- **More connections.** New Claude connectors and ready-made skills for local services.
- **Google Sheets and Google Docs without Office.**

## Not yet

- Macs with Intel processors.
- Linux.
- Teo working while the computer is off.
