# Support

[Русская версия](SUPPORT.md) · [Back to README](README.en.md)

**Something does not work or is unclear?**

1. See the [FAQ](docs/FAQ.en.md). It covers price and payment, Windows and macOS warnings, the microphone, uninstalling and checking the file.
2. Press "Send feedback" («Оставить отзыв») in Teo: Settings («Настройки»), the "Support" block («Поддержка»). Send your ideas there too.
3. Write by email: [saint4aiinst@gmail.com](mailto:saint4aiinst@gmail.com). Give your account number: you find it on the [teo.onai.academy/account](https://teo.onai.academy/account) page.

Do not attach keys, passwords or private chats.

Vulnerabilities: [SECURITY.en.md](SECURITY.en.md).
